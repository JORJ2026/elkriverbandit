**Full description**
Elk River Bandit is a fast, funny arcade game starring ten bandit animals — Rita Raccoon, Pogo, Mochi, Quackers and more.
Race the river in the Infinite Runner, hop through 10 Platformer levels, or explore 60 maps in Free Run. Collect loot, dodge hazards,
slide, spin and throw trash — all to a shuffled soundtrack of 21 songs.

Spelling mode turns any word list into a game: paste your own words (great for parents and teachers) and grab the glowing letters in order.
There is also a Watch · Hide · Spell test.

• 3 game styles, 60 maps, 10 characters, Easy / Normal / Hard
• Spelling mode with your own word lists
• 21 songs — shuffle or pick your favourite
• Stats and achievements
• Works offline • No ads • No accounts • No tracking
